Who We Are

About AIX

Accelerating AI eXcellence

The Artificial Intelligence Transformation Centre (AIX) is a national collaborative initiative dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence innovation and adoption across Malaysia.

Located at MMU's Cyberjaya and Melaka campuses, AIX houses 13 specialised laboratories equipped with advanced technologies and digital infrastructure that support research, experimentation, solution development, industry collaboration, and talent development.

By bringing together government agencies, academia, industry leaders, researchers, startups, and innovators, AIX creates an ecosystem where ideas are transformed into practical AI solutions that address real-world challenges.

Whether developing next-generation technologies, validating AI applications, or nurturing future AI talent, AIX serves as a catalyst for Malaysia's digital economy.

Our Facilities

Our AI Laboratories

13 Specialised Laboratories

AIX comprises thirteen specialised laboratories, each focusing on strategic areas of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

10 labs at MMU Cyberjaya 3 labs at MMU Melaka

GAIA

Generative AI for Advanced Innovation

A flagship AI laboratory providing hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing (NLP), machine vision, cloud computing, and embedded AI systems.

MMU Cyberjaya

XR-AI

Extended Reality and Artificial Intelligence

A state-of-the-art facility that integrates Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), Mixed Reality (MR), and AI technologies to support immersive learning, research, and innovation.

MMU Cyberjaya

Intelligent Robotics

A laboratory dedicated to robotics and intelligent systems, enabling hands-on learning, experimentation, and development of robotic hardware and AI-powered robotic applications.

MMU Cyberjaya

Game Tech

An innovation hub that combines AI and game technologies to develop interactive digital experiences, intelligent gaming solutions, and next-generation applications.

MMU Cyberjaya

NextGen Data Analytics

A data analytics laboratory equipped with advanced computing infrastructure, edge AI technologies, AIoT systems, cloud platforms, and large-scale datasets to support artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, and data-driven decision-making.

MMU Melaka

GEMA

Generative Multi-Dimensional Audio AI

An immersive audio laboratory equipped with advanced spatial audio technologies, including Ambisonics and Dolby Atmos systems, to explore the convergence of audio, AI, and spatial computing.

MMU Cyberjaya

Digital Forensic

A specialised laboratory that leverages AI-driven tools to support digital evidence analysis, cybercrime investigations, and cybersecurity research.

MMU Cyberjaya

Business Intelligence

A data analytics and business intelligence laboratory that leverages AI-driven insights, predictive analytics, data storytelling and advanced visualization technologies to transform complex data into actionable business intelligence, enabling data-driven decision-making, operational excellence, and digital transformation.

MMU Melaka

SIGAAL

Sixth-Generation Mobile with AI Assistance

A next-generation telecommunications laboratory focused on 6G technologies, terahertz (THz) systems, and AI-enabled network innovation aligned with Malaysia's future connectivity agenda.

MMU Cyberjaya

BlockSphere

A specialised laboratory dedicated to advancing secure, transparent, and trustworthy digital ecosystems through the integration of artificial intelligence with blockchain, smart contracts, applied cryptography, and decentralized data systems. The lab enables end-to-end innovation from cryptographic protocol design, zero-knowledge proof systems, and smart contract security analysis to decentralized application development, blockchain-IoT integration, and trusted data validation, supporting the translation of research ideas into secure, verifiable, and field-ready decentralized solutions.

MMU Cyberjaya

Cyber Range

A cybersecurity simulation and training environment that supports AI-enabled cyber defence, threat intelligence, attack simulation, and security operations to strengthen digital resilience.

MMU Cyberjaya

xIoT

Extended IoT Security Research

A special lab dedicated to securing and advancing intelligent Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystems through AI-powered threat analysis, real-time intrusion detection, vulnerability assessment, and practical cybersecurity investigations. The lab enables end-to-end innovation from edge-AI model development and embedded system integration to device enclosure, hardware refinement, and prototype packaging, supporting the translation of research ideas into functional, field-ready IoT solutions.

MMU Cyberjaya

StatLab

Data Analysis Research

An innovative research laboratory specializing in data science, statistics, and AI-powered analytics, delivering evidence-based solutions and strategic insights through advanced quantitative and qualitative research methodologies.
